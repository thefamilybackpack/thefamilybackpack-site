First, reset expectations. Milwaukee isn't a Wisconsin Dells-style waterpark-hotel town; its family lodging is downtown hotels with indoor pools, walkable to the lakefront and museums. That's actually good news with kids, because the best base here isn't a resort you hide inside, it's a hotel you walk out of. Sort the options by how your family travels.

Our pick · Best valueDrury Plaza Downtown

The indoor pool at a downtown hotel

For most families we'd book the Drury Plaza Downtown, and the reason is the math. On top of an indoor pool and a large hot tub, it includes a free hot breakfast, a free evening “Kickback” with food and drinks, and all-day popcorn, plus free cribs. With kids, “free breakfast and dinner” isn't a frill, it can quietly cover two of your three daily meals, and the spacious suites with a separate living room give everyone room to spread out. It's walkable to the theaters and the RiverWalk, and consistently one of the top-rated family hotels in the city.

From our family · travelling with a one-year-old

The free-cribs-and-meals combo is exactly what makes a place easy with a baby. We're not hunting for breakfast with a hungry one-year-old, we're eating downstairs in pajamas, and the evening Kickback means we don't have to march a tired toddler to a restaurant at night. An indoor pool to burn off the last of the day's energy before bed is the final piece.

The classicThe Pfister, for a special stay

If the trip is a celebration, the historic Pfister Hotel is Milwaukee's grand old dame: a warm indoor pool with skyline views and a walk to the lakefront, museums and the Public Market. It runs pricier than the Drury and doesn't throw in the free meals, but for a milestone trip the character is worth it. Families who want walkability without the splurge do well at the Courtyard Downtown (heated pool, steps from Third Street Market Hall's food stalls and arcade) or the budget-friendly, stylish Cambria in the Third Ward.

If the kids insistThe waterpark option

A splash zone at a suburban waterpark hotel

Milwaukee proper doesn't have a waterpark hotel, but if that's what your kids are picturing, Springs Water Park at The Ingleside Hotel in nearby Pewaukee (just west off I-94) has slides, splash zones and a lazy river, the classic Wisconsin waterpark-weekend experience about 20 minutes out. It's the move for a birthday or a staycation, though you trade the walkable downtown for a suburban base. For the full version, the Wisconsin Dells (about two hours west) is the waterpark capital.

Before you book

What is the best family hotel in Milwaukee?

For most families, the Drury Plaza Downtown: an indoor pool and hot tub, free hot breakfast, a free evening Kickback with food and drinks, free cribs, and suites with a separate living room, which together save a lot with kids. For a special-occasion stay, the historic Pfister; for budget, the Cambria in the Third Ward.

Does Milwaukee have a waterpark hotel?

Not in the city itself. The nearest is Springs Water Park at The Ingleside Hotel in Pewaukee, about 20 minutes west off I-94, with slides, splash zones and a lazy river. For the full waterpark experience, the Wisconsin Dells (about two hours west) is the state's waterpark capital.

Where should families stay in Milwaukee?

Downtown is the sweet spot: you can walk to the lakefront, the art museum, Discovery World and the Public Market. Look for a hotel with an indoor pool, and prioritise walkability over luxury. The Drury Plaza, Courtyard Downtown and Cambria are all walkable family picks; the Pfister is the upscale option.

Do Milwaukee hotels have indoor pools?

Yes, most downtown family hotels do, including the Drury Plaza, the Pfister, the Courtyard Downtown and the Cambria, and they're open year-round (hours can vary by season or event). An indoor pool is worth prioritising with kids, it's the reliable way to burn off energy before bed whatever the weather.
