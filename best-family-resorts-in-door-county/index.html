Door County, Wisconsin · Where to stay

The best family resorts in Door County

Door County isn't a waterpark-resort town like the Dells. Its family lodging is suites with kitchens, indoor pools as a weather hedge, and a few genuine all-in-one resorts. Here's how the real options compare, and which one we'd book.

First, reset expectations. If you're picturing a Wisconsin Dells-style indoor waterpark resort, Door County doesn't really do that. What it does well is family suites with kitchens, most with an indoor pool for when the lake wind kills the beach day, in walkable harbor towns. The best "resort" here is the one that fits how your family actually travels, so let's sort them by that.

The all-rounder · where we’d startNewport Resort, Egg Harbor

The heated indoor pool at Newport

Newport is the one we'd book for most families. It's a 59-suite resort in the center of Egg Harbor with one- and two-bedroom suites, each with a full kitchen, fireplace and living area, and it has both an indoor and an outdoor heated pool plus indoor and outdoor playgrounds. Crucially it's a walk from Egg Harbor's shops and restaurants, so you're not driving for every meal, and it's open all year. It consistently rates near the top of Door County family lodging for a reason.

From our family · travelling with a one-year-old

With a baby, the two things that make or break a resort for us are a kitchen and an indoor pool, and Newport has both. The kitchen means we're not marching a tired one-year-old into a restaurant three times a day, and the indoor pool saves the afternoon when the wind comes off the bay and the beach is a write-off. A walkable town is the third quiet win: nap in the room, walk out for dinner, no car seat battle.

The big all-in-oneThe Landmark Resort, Egg Harbor

If you'd rather barely leave the property, the Landmark is the peninsula's largest resort: 294 suites on a bluff over Green Bay, with five pools (indoor and outdoor), whirlpools, tennis and pickleball, and an on-site restaurant. It's a self-contained family destination, which is exactly its appeal and its trade-off. You get everything on site, but it's a short drive from town and it runs pricier than Newport. Great for a group or a family that wants a home base with its own amenities; less charming if you came for walkable harbor-town life. (Note their indoor pool has a short annual maintenance closure in late February.)

Best valueWestwood Shores, Sturgeon Bay

The waterfront at Westwood Shores

Sturgeon Bay is the gateway city, less quaint than the northern towns but noticeably cheaper and open year-round, and Westwood Shores is its best family value: a waterfront resort with an indoor pool and hot tub and consistently high guest ratings. You trade the postcard harbor-town setting for a lower nightly rate and a location that's the first thing you reach coming up the peninsula. A smart pick if the budget's tight or you're using Door County as one stop on a bigger trip.

BudgetOpen Hearth Lodge, Sister Bay

If you just need a clean, cheap base with a pool and don't care about kitchens or frills, Open Hearth Lodge in Sister Bay does that and does it well, with some of the highest guest ratings on the peninsula for a simple lodge. No kitchen and no playground, but it's walkable to Sister Bay (and the goats), has an indoor pool, and costs the least of the four. The right call for a short stay where you're out all day anyway.

Before you book

What is the best family resort in Door County?

It depends on what your family wants. For an easy all-rounder, Newport Resort in Egg Harbor has kitchens, indoor and outdoor pools, playgrounds and a walkable location. For a big do-everything base, the Landmark has five pools and 294 suites. For value, Westwood Shores in Sturgeon Bay is cheaper and waterfront. For a simple, cheap stay, Open Hearth Lodge in Sister Bay. Match the resort to your trip rather than chasing a single “best.”

Does Door County have a waterpark resort like the Dells?

Not really. Door County's family lodging is suites-with-kitchens and indoor pools rather than big indoor waterparks. If a waterpark is the main event for your kids, the Wisconsin Dells (about two hours southwest) is the better fit; Door County is for families who want the outdoors, beaches and small towns.

Which Door County town should families stay in?

Egg Harbor is the most resort-dense and central, and the easiest base with young kids. Fish Creek is walkable and next to Peninsula State Park. Sister Bay is a little quieter with the goats and a good beach. Sturgeon Bay is the cheaper gateway city. Pick the town first, then the property.

Do I need a kitchen and an indoor pool?

With kids in Wisconsin, both help a lot. A kitchen means flexible meals around nap times and early hikes instead of three restaurant trips a day. An indoor pool is a genuine weather hedge, since lake wind can cancel a beach day fast. Newport Resort and the Landmark both have kitchens and indoor pools.

When should I book a Door County resort?

Book the popular Egg Harbor and Fish Creek pool resorts by spring for a July or August trip; summer weekends sell out and rates climb. Late September is easier to book and cheaper, with fall colour, though it's too cold to swim outdoors. Winter has the widest availability and lowest rates.
