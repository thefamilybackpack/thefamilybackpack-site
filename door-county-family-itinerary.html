Door County, Wisconsin · The plan

A Door County itinerary, day by day

Here is a paced three-to-four-day plan for Door County with kids, built around one base town so you are not racing the peninsula. Slot in the beach, the ferry, the goats and a fish boil, and leave room for the day to breathe.

The short answerTL;DR

Base in Fish Creek or Sister Bay and plan roughly this: Day 1 Peninsula State Park and a bay beach; Day 2 the Washington Island ferry; Day 3 the goats, custard and a fish boil, with a slow morning. Add a Day 4 to spread it out or add Cave Point. Do not try to see the whole 70-mile peninsula; one base and short drives is the whole trick.

The single best decision you make in Door County is to stop trying to see all of it. The peninsula runs about 70 miles on two-lane roads, and a family that bases in one town and takes short drives out will have a far better trip than one chasing the far ends. Pick Fish Creek (central, walkable, next to Peninsula State Park) or Sister Bay (a little quieter, with the goats), and build days out from there.

Day 1 · Settle inPeninsula State Park and a beach

The shore road through Peninsula State Park

Ease in with the park on your doorstep. Spend the morning in Peninsula State Park: drive the shore road, climb (or ramp up) Eagle Tower for the view over Green Bay, and let the kids run the easy trails. In the afternoon, drop down to Nicolet Beach, the park's calm, shallow bay beach, for a swim and a paddle-rental if you are up for it. You will need a Wisconsin State Park sticker for the day. Keep dinner easy and close; you have got bigger days coming.

Day 2 · The big outingThe Washington Island ferry

Give a full day to the island. Drive to Northport Pier at the tip of the peninsula and take the ferry across Death's Door (about 30 minutes; 2026 fares are $15 adult, $8 for kids 6–11, under 6 free). Bring the car, the island is seven miles across. Once there, Schoolhouse Beach's smooth limestone and a picnic fill an easy afternoon. It is a full half-day with travel, so do not bolt anything big onto the evening, just custard and an early night.

From our family · travelling with a one-year-old

With a baby, we treat the ferry day as the whole day and expect nothing else from it. The crossing itself is the entertainment (stand out on the open deck), naps happen in the car between stops, and we keep the island afternoon to one easy beach. Trying to add a hike or a second town on ferry day is how you get a meltdown at 5pm.

Day 3 · The greatest hitsGoats, custard and a fish boil

Goats on the roof at Al Johnson’s

Slow the morning, then collect the classics. Have breakfast at Al Johnson's in Sister Bay and watch the goats on the roof. Spend the middle of the day however the weather points, another beach, a town wander, a cherry orchard in July, then book a fish boil for the evening (the White Gull Inn in Fish Creek is the classic) and arrive early for the boil-over fireball. Finish with custard at Wilson's or Not Licked Yet. That is Door County in a day.

Day 4, if you have it

A fourth day is for breathing room, not cramming. Use it to spread the first three days out, or add Cave Point County Park on the Lake Michigan side for its dramatic bluffs (a look, not a swim with little kids), a cherry-picking stop in season, or simply a slow beach day. With kids, the day you leave unplanned is often the one everyone remembers.

Ferry fares, park stickers and seasonal hours checked Sep 2026. The goats graze roughly late spring to mid-October; confirm ferry schedules and fish-boil nights before you lock the plan.

Before you go

How many days do you need in Door County with kids?

Three to four days is ideal. Three covers Peninsula State Park and a beach, a Washington Island ferry day, and a day of the goats, custard and a fish boil. A fourth day gives you breathing room or a Cave Point add-on. Base in one town so you are not driving the whole 70-mile peninsula.

What is the best Door County itinerary for families?

Base in Fish Creek or Sister Bay. Day 1: Peninsula State Park and Nicolet Beach. Day 2: the Washington Island ferry and Schoolhouse Beach. Day 3: breakfast with the goats at Al Johnson's, an easy afternoon, and a fish boil at night. Add a Day 4 to slow the pace or add Cave Point.

Should you move hotels around Door County?

No, with kids it is better to base in one town and take short drives out. The peninsula is long and the roads are slow, so packing and re-checking-in eats time you would rather spend at the beach. Pick a central base like Fish Creek and day-trip from there.

What should you not miss in Door County with kids?

Peninsula State Park (Eagle Tower and Nicolet Beach), the Washington Island ferry across Death's Door, the goats on the roof at Al Johnson's, and a fish boil with its dramatic boil-over. Fit those four into three or four unhurried days and you have done Door County right.
