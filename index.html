Who we are

A family that travels the Midwest, slowly, and with a toddler.

We’re a family based in the Upper Midwest, and we plan trips the slow way: reading everything, calling ahead to confirm the hours and prices, and cutting the noise down to the version we’d give a friend. We travel with a one-year-old, so we care about the things other guides skip, shade, naps, kitchens, and whether the stroller actually makes it.

We don’t claim to have been everywhere. When we’ve been, we tell you. When we’re curating, we say so, and we check every fact against the official source before it goes up.
